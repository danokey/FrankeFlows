Intention

This retreat is an invitation to unplug, laugh, breathe deeply, and come home to ourselves. Surrounded by forest, fresh air, and wide open space, we gather at Nectar to slow down, move our bodies, share good food, and enjoy meaningful connection. This is time to listen inward, be supported by community, and remember the joy of simply being present. Through yoga, nature, and shared experience, we create space for rest, renewal, and genuine connection—leaving feeling lighter, more grounded, and deeply nourished.

Daily Schedule

Expect to begin each day with an invigorating Vinyasa flow filled with breath, movement, and joy. Then in the evenings embark on a journey inward for a Yin/Restorative practice to help release tension and process insights of your day.

In between yoga practices find adventure or rest. There will be plenty of free time to explore bowen island, take a hike, schedule a massage, or add on experiences/activities.

Cuisine

MIST Thermal Experience

Included for all retreat participants, Mist Thermal Sanctuary is a 90-minute hot-cold contrast circuit.

Mist Circuit Includes:

*Facilities: Outdoor hot/cold shower, wood-fired sauna, cold plunge, fire-warmed rest area
*Amenities: Robes, towels, slippers, Mist botanical body care, herbal tea
*Guidance: Gentle chimes every 15 minutes support intuitive transitions
